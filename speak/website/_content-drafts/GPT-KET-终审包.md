### 话题 1:假期去哪玩 — Holidays and travel
`ket-holidays`

1. `[♂Tom]` Do you like going on holiday, Lily?
   > 你喜欢去度假吗，莉莉？
2. `[♀Lily]` Yes, I love it! I went to the beach last summer.
   > 喜欢！去年夏天我去了海边。
3. `[♂Tom]` That sounds fun. What did you do there?
   > 听起来很好玩。你在那儿做了什么？
4. `[♀Lily]` I swam in the sea and built sandcastles with my sister.
   > 我在海里游泳，还和妹妹堆了沙堡。
5. `[♂Tom]` Nice! Do you prefer the beach or the mountains?
   > 真好！你更喜欢海边还是山里？
6. `[♀Lily]` The beach, definitely. What about you, Tom?
   > 肯定是海边。你呢，汤姆？
7. `[♂Tom]` I like the mountains. I went skiing there last winter.
   > 我喜欢山里。去年冬天我去那儿滑雪了。
8. `[♀Lily]` Wow, that sounds exciting! I'd love to try skiing.
   > 哇，听起来好刺激！我也想试试滑雪。

**考法**:**考法** 假期旅行是 KET 高频话题。考官常问 Do you like...? / Where did you go...? / What did you do...? 回答用一般现在时说喜好、一般过去时说经历,给 1-2 句即可。

**万能句型**:
- `I went to ___ last ___.` — 说去过的地方和时间
- `I prefer ___ because ___.` — 说偏好和原因
- `That sounds ___!` — 回应对方(fun/exciting)

### 话题 2:怎么去 — Getting around
`ket-transport`

1. `[♂Tom]` How do you get to school, Lily?
   > 你怎么去学校，莉莉？
2. `[♀Lily]` I usually go by bus. It takes about twenty minutes.
   > 我一般坐公交，大概二十分钟。
3. `[♂Tom]` Do you ever walk or ride a bike?
   > 你走路或骑车去过吗？
4. `[♀Lily]` Sometimes I ride my bike when the weather is nice.
   > 天气好的时候我有时会骑自行车。
5. `[♂Tom]` What's your favourite way to travel?
   > 你最喜欢的出行方式是什么？
6. `[♀Lily]` I love taking the train because I can look out of the window.
   > 我喜欢坐火车，因为可以看窗外的风景。
7. `[♂Tom]` Me too. How do you get to the city centre?
   > 我也是。你怎么去市中心？
8. `[♀Lily]` I take the underground. It's fast and never gets stuck in traffic.
   > 我坐地铁。又快，还不会堵车。

**考法**:**考法** 交通出行是 KET 常考。考官问 How do you get to...? / How long does it take? 回答用 I go by bus / on foot,时间用 It takes about... 简单直接即可。

**万能句型**:
- `I usually go by ___.` — 说交通方式
- `It takes about ___ minutes.` — 说花多久
- `I like taking the ___ because ___.` — 说喜欢的方式和原因

### 话题 3:喜欢的运动 — Sports and games
`ket-sports`

1. `[♂Tom]` What sports do you like, Lily?
   > 你喜欢什么运动，莉莉？
2. `[♀Lily]` My favourite sport is swimming. I go to the pool every weekend.
   > 我最喜欢游泳。我每个周末都去泳池。
3. `[♂Tom]` That's great. Do you play any team sports?
   > 真棒。你玩团队运动吗？
4. `[♀Lily]` Yes, I play basketball with my friends after school.
   > 玩，我放学后和朋友打篮球。
5. `[♂Tom]` How often do you practise?
   > 你多久练一次？
6. `[♀Lily]` About twice a week. I'm not very good, but it's really fun.
   > 大概一周两次。我打得不太好，但很有意思。
7. `[♂Tom]` Do you like watching sports on TV too?
   > 你也喜欢在电视上看比赛吗？
8. `[♀Lily]` Sometimes. I love watching football with my dad.
   > 有时候。我喜欢和爸爸一起看足球。

**考法**:**考法** 运动是 KET 高频话题。考官问 What sports do you like? / How often do you...? 回答用现在时说喜好和频率(once a week / every day),给个理由更好。

**万能句型**:
- `My favourite sport is ___.` — 说最爱的运动
- `I play ___ ___ times a week.` — 说频率
- `I'm not very good at ___, but it's fun.` — 说不擅长但喜欢

### 话题 4:过节啦 — Festivals and celebrations
`ket-festivals`

1. `[♂Tom]` What's your favourite festival, Lily?
   > 你最喜欢的节日是什么，莉莉？
2. `[♀Lily]` I love Spring Festival. The whole family gets together.
   > 我喜欢春节。全家人都会团聚。
3. `[♂Tom]` That sounds lovely. What do you usually do?
   > 听起来真好。你们通常做什么？
4. `[♀Lily]` We have a big dinner and my grandma gives us red packets.
   > 我们吃一顿大餐，奶奶还会给我们红包。
5. `[♂Tom]` Nice! Do you eat any special food?
   > 真好！你们吃什么特别的食物吗？
6. `[♀Lily]` Yes, we make dumplings together. They're my favourite.
   > 会的，我们一起包饺子。那是我的最爱。
7. `[♀Lily]` Which festival do you like, Tom?
   > 你喜欢哪个节日，汤姆？
8. `[♂Tom]` I like Mid-Autumn Festival. We eat mooncakes and watch the moon.
   > 我喜欢中秋节。我们吃月饼，赏月亮。

**考法**:**考法** 节日庆祝是 KET 常考话题。考官问 What's your favourite festival? / How do you celebrate? 回答用现在时说习俗(we eat.../ we visit...),简单具体即可。

**万能句型**:
- `My favourite festival is ___.` — 说最爱的节日
- `We usually ___ together.` — 说怎么庆祝
- `It's my favourite because ___.` — 说喜欢的原因

### 话题 5:我的好朋友 — My friends
`ket-friends`

1. `[♂Tom]` Tell me about your best friend, Lily.
   > 跟我说说你最好的朋友吧，莉莉。
2. `[♀Lily]` My best friend is Emma. We've known each other since we were six.
   > 我最好的朋友是艾玛。我们六岁就认识了。
3. `[♂Tom]` What do you like doing together?
   > 你们喜欢一起做什么？
4. `[♀Lily]` We love drawing and watching cartoons at the weekend.
   > 我们喜欢周末一起画画、看动画片。
5. `[♂Tom]` She sounds fun. What's she like?
   > 她听起来很有意思。她是什么样的人？
6. `[♀Lily]` She's really kind and always makes me laugh.
   > 她非常善良，总能逗我笑。
7. `[♂Tom]` How often do you see each other?
   > 你们多久见一次？
8. `[♀Lily]` Almost every day at school. We sit next to each other.
   > 几乎每天在学校见。我们坐在一起。

**考法**:**考法** 朋友是 KET 高频话题。考官问 Tell me about your best friend / What do you do together? 回答用现在时描述朋友(She's kind / funny)和一起做的事,给 1-2 句即可。

**万能句型**:
- `My best friend is ___.` — 介绍好朋友
- `We like ___ together.` — 说一起做什么
- `She's really ___ and ___.` — 描述性格

`ket-jobs`

1. `[♂Tom]` What do you want to be when you grow up, Lily?
   > 你长大想做什么，莉莉？
2. `[♀Lily]` I want to be a doctor. I'd like to help sick people.
   > 我想当医生。我想帮助生病的人。
3. `[♂Tom]` That's a great job. Why do you want to do it?
   > 那是个很棒的工作。你为什么想做呢？
4. `[♀Lily]` Because my mum is a nurse, and I want to help people like her.
   > 因为我妈妈是护士，我想像她一样帮助别人。
5. `[♂Tom]` What's your favourite subject at school?
   > 你在学校最喜欢的科目是什么？
6. `[♀Lily]` I love science. It helps me understand how things work.
   > 我喜欢科学。它帮我理解事物是怎么运作的。
7. `[♀Lily]` What about you, Tom? What do you want to be?
   > 你呢，汤姆？你想做什么？
8. `[♂Tom]` I want to be a pilot. I've always dreamed of flying planes.
   > 我想当飞行员。我一直梦想着开飞机。

**考法**:**考法** 未来工作是 KET 常考话题。考官问 What do you want to be? / Why? 回答用 I want to be a... because... 说梦想和简单理由,现在时即可。

**万能句型**:
- `I want to be a ___.` — 说想做的工作
- `I want to do this because ___.` — 说原因
- `My favourite subject is ___.` — 说喜欢的科目
