### 话题 1:人际关系与友谊 — Relationships and Friendship
`fce-relationships`

**Part 1 · 考官问 / 考生答**

1. `[考官问]` Do you have a large group of friends, or just a few close ones?
   > 你朋友很多,还是只有几个亲密的?
2. `[考生答]` I'd say I have a small circle of close friends rather than lots of acquaintances. I find it much easier to be myself around people who really know me.
   > 我会说我有一小圈亲密朋友,而不是一堆泛泛之交。在真正了解我的人面前,我更容易做自己。
3. `[考官问]` How important is it to you to keep in touch with old friends?
   > 和老朋友保持联系对你有多重要?
4. `[考生答]` It matters a great deal to me. Even if we don't speak for months, there's a real comfort in knowing they'll still be there when it counts.
   > 对我非常重要。即使几个月不联系,一想到关键时刻他们仍会在,就让人很安心。
5. `[考官问]` Do you think it's possible to make genuine friends online?
   > 你觉得能在网上交到真心朋友吗?
6. `[考生答]` Absolutely, though it does take time. I've met people through shared hobbies online who've become just as close as friends I see in person.
   > 当然,不过确实需要时间。我在网上因共同爱好认识的人,有些已经和我线下的朋友一样亲近。
7. `[考官问]` What do you think makes someone a good friend?
   > 你觉得什么样的人算好朋友?
8. `[考生答]` For me, it comes down to trust and being a good listener. A real friend sticks around when things get difficult, not just when it's fun.
   > 对我来说,关键在于信任和会倾听。真朋友在困难时也不离开,而不只是在开心时才在。

**Part 2 · 考生看图独白**

1. Both photographs show people spending time together, but the nature of their relationships looks quite different.
   > 两张照片都展示人们共处,但关系的性质看起来很不一样。
2. In the first picture, two friends are sitting in a café, deep in conversation and clearly enjoying each other's company.
   > 第一张照片里,两个朋友坐在咖啡馆里,聊得很投入,显然很享受彼此的陪伴。
3. The second image, by contrast, shows a large group at what looks like a party, all laughing and celebrating together.
   > 相比之下,第二张展示的是一大群人在类似聚会的场合,一起说笑、庆祝。
4. The pair in the café might be catching up after a long time apart, sharing something personal that matters to them both.
   > 咖啡馆里那两个人可能是久别重逢在叙旧,分享着对彼此都重要的私事。
5. On the other hand, the group in the second photo seem more focused on having fun collectively than on any one-to-one connection.
   > 另一方面,第二张里的那群人似乎更在意集体的欢乐,而非一对一的交流。
6. I imagine people value both kinds of relationship, because we need close friends to confide in as well as a wider circle to enjoy ourselves with.
   > 我想人们对两种关系都很看重,因为我们既需要能倾诉的密友,也需要一个能一起玩乐的更大圈子。
7. Personally, I'd say the quieter, one-to-one friendship looks more meaningful to me, since that's usually where the deepest bonds are formed.
   > 就我个人而言,那种安静的一对一友谊对我更有意义,因为最深的情谊通常正是在那里建立的。

**考法提示**:**考法** 人际关系是 FCE 高频话题。Part 1 考官会问你的交友方式、如何维系老友、什么样才算好朋友,回答给出 2 句左右并说明理由(常用现在时+现在完成时)。Part 2 常给「两人独处 vs 群体聚会」的对比图,用 whereas / on the other hand 对比,结尾用 Personally 表个人看法。

**万能句型**:
- `I'd say I have ___ rather than ___.` — 澄清交友倾向
- `It matters a great deal to me because ___.` — 强调重要性
- `For me, it comes down to ___.` — 总结关键要素
- `Personally, I'd say ___, since ___.` — 表个人观点

### 话题 2:教育与学习 — Education and Learning
`fce-education`

**Part 1 · 考官问 / 考生答**

1. `[考官问]` How do you prefer to study — on your own or with others?
   > 你更喜欢怎么学习——自己学还是和别人一起?
2. `[考生答]` It really depends on the subject, but on the whole I learn best on my own, in a quiet place where I can focus without any distractions.
   > 这其实要看科目,但总体上我一个人在安静、没干扰的地方学得最好。
3. `[考官问]` Do you think exams are a fair way to measure ability?
   > 你觉得考试是衡量能力的公平方式吗?
4. `[考生答]` Not entirely, to be honest. They show how well you perform under pressure, but they don't always capture creativity or practical skills.
   > 老实说,不完全公平。考试能看出你在压力下的表现,却未必能反映创造力或实际能力。
5. `[考官问]` Have you ever learned something useful outside of school?
   > 你有没有在学校之外学到过有用的东西?
6. `[考生答]` Definitely. I taught myself to edit videos through online tutorials, and it's turned out to be far more useful than I ever expected.
   > 当然。我通过网上教程自学了剪辑视频,结果发现它比我预想的有用得多。
7. `[考官问]` How important do you think it is to keep learning as an adult?
   > 你觉得成年后继续学习有多重要?
8. `[考生答]` I'd say it's essential these days. The world changes so quickly that if you stop learning, you can fall behind before you even realise it.
   > 我觉得如今这是必需的。世界变化太快,你一旦停止学习,可能还没察觉就已经落后了。

**Part 2 · 考生看图独白**

1. Both pictures show people learning, but they're going about it in very different settings.
   > 两张照片都展示人们在学习,但方式和环境很不一样。
2. In the first one, a class of students is listening to a teacher, who seems to be explaining something at the front of the room.
   > 第一张里,一班学生在听老师讲课,老师似乎正在教室前面讲解着什么。
3. The second picture, by contrast, shows a young person studying alone at home, following a lesson on their laptop.
   > 相比之下,第二张展示的是一个年轻人独自在家,跟着笔记本电脑上的课程学习。
4. The students in the classroom have the advantage of asking questions directly and learning from one another.
   > 教室里的学生有个好处,就是能直接提问、彼此学习。
5. On the other hand, the person studying online can work at their own pace and revisit anything they find difficult.
   > 另一方面,在线学习的人可以按自己的节奏来,任何觉得难的地方都能反复看。
6. I think both approaches are valuable, since some people thrive on interaction while others concentrate better in their own space.
   > 我觉得两种方式各有价值,有些人在互动中如鱼得水,另一些人则在自己的空间里更专注。
7. Personally, I'd choose a mix of the two, because the discipline of a classroom combined with the flexibility of online study would suit me best.
   > 就我个人而言,我会选两者结合,因为课堂的纪律加上在线学习的灵活最适合我。

**考法提示**:**考法** 教育是 FCE 常考话题。Part 1 考官会问你喜欢的学习方式、考试是否重要、学校教育的价值,回答要给理由和例子。Part 2 常给「课堂上课 vs 独自在线学习」的对比图,用 whereas / while 对比两种方式的利弊,结尾表明个人偏好。

**万能句型**:
- `I learn best when ___.` — 说学习方式
- `There's a lot to be said for ___.` — 肯定某种做法
- `While ___ suits some people, I personally prefer ___.` — 对比后表偏好
- `At the end of the day, what matters most is ___.` — 归纳核心

### 话题 3:理想与未来 — Ambitions and the Future
`fce-ambitions`

**Part 1 · 考官问 / 考生答**

1. `[考官问]` Do you have a clear idea of what you'd like to do in the future?
   > 你对未来想做什么有清晰的想法吗?
2. `[考生答]` Roughly, yes. In the long run I'd like to work in something creative, though I'm still keeping my options open for now.
   > 大致有。长远来看我想做点有创造性的工作,不过目前我还在保留各种可能。
3. `[考官问]` Is earning a lot of money important to you?
   > 赚很多钱对你重要吗?
4. `[考生答]` It's nice to be comfortable, of course, but what matters more to me than money is doing something I actually find meaningful.
   > 生活宽裕当然好,但对我来说比钱更重要的,是做一件我真正觉得有意义的事。
5. `[考官问]` If you could live anywhere in the world, where would it be?
   > 如果你能住在世界任何地方,会选哪里?
6. `[考生答]` If I had the chance, I'd live somewhere by the coast. Being near the sea has always helped me think clearly and feel more relaxed.
   > 如果有机会,我想住在海边。靠近大海总能让我思路清晰、更放松。
7. `[考官问]` How would you define a successful life?
   > 你会怎样定义成功的人生?
8. `[考生答]` For me, success means having good relationships and being content with what I do, rather than simply climbing to the top of a career ladder.
   > 对我而言,成功是拥有好的人际关系、对自己所做的事感到满足,而不只是爬到职业阶梯的顶端。

**Part 2 · 考生看图独白**

1. Both images seem to capture people at an important turning point in their lives, but their choices look very different.
   > 两张照片似乎都捕捉到人们人生的重要转折点,但他们的选择看起来很不一样。
2. In the first picture, a young woman in an office looks settled and confident, as though she's built a stable career.
   > 第一张里,一位年轻女性在办公室里显得安稳自信,仿佛已经建立起稳定的事业。
3. The second one, on the other hand, shows someone travelling with a backpack, apparently chasing a very different kind of dream.
   > 第二张则展示一个背着背包旅行的人,显然在追逐一种截然不同的梦想。
4. The woman in the office may well value security and a steady income, which allows her to plan ahead with confidence.
   > 办公室里的女性很可能看重安全感和稳定收入,这让她能有底气地规划未来。
5. In contrast, the traveller seems willing to take risks and gather experiences, even if it means giving up a certain amount of stability.
   > 相比之下,那位旅行者似乎愿意冒险、去积累经历,哪怕这意味着放弃一定的稳定。
6. If I'm honest, I can see the appeal of both, since we all have to balance our dreams against the need to feel secure.
   > 说实话,两者的吸引力我都能理解,因为我们都得在梦想和安全感之间寻找平衡。
7. Personally, I'd lean towards the traveller's path while I'm young, because I believe experiences shape us far more than possessions ever could.
   > 就我个人而言,趁年轻我更倾向旅行者的道路,因为我相信经历对人的塑造远胜过任何财物。

**考法提示**:**考法** 理想与未来是 FCE 常考话题。Part 1 考官会问你的未来计划、想从事的工作、如何定义成功,回答多用 would like to / hope to / be planning to,并给理由。Part 2 常给「稳定工作 vs 追逐梦想」的对比图,用条件句(If...)设想不同选择,结尾表明自己的价值取向。

**万能句型**:
- `In the long run, I'd like to ___.` — 谈长远目标
- `What matters more to me than money is ___.` — 谈价值排序
- `If I had the chance, I would ___.` — 条件句设想
- `Success, to me, means ___ rather than ___.` — 定义成功

### 话题 4:生活方式与节奏 — Lifestyle and Daily Habits
`fce-lifestyle`

**Part 1 · 考官问 / 考生答**

1. `[考官问]` Are you more of an early riser or a night owl?
   > 你更像早起的人还是夜猫子?
2. `[考生答]` Definitely a night owl, I'm afraid. I do my best thinking late at night, though I know it isn't the healthiest routine.
   > 恐怕绝对是夜猫子。我在深夜思路最清晰,尽管我知道这不是最健康的作息。
3. `[考官问]` How do you usually balance work or study with relaxing?
   > 你通常怎么平衡工作学习和放松?
4. `[考生答]` I try to strike a balance by setting clear cut-off times. Once I stop for the day, I make a point of switching off completely.
   > 我靠设定明确的收工时间来平衡。一旦当天结束,我会刻意让自己彻底放松下来。
5. `[考官问]` Is there a habit you'd like to change?
   > 有没有你想改掉的习惯?
6. `[考生答]` One habit I'd love to change is checking my phone first thing in the morning. It sets a restless tone for the whole day.
   > 我很想改掉的一个习惯是一早醒来就看手机。它给一整天定下了焦躁的基调。
7. `[考官问]` Do you think people's lifestyles are healthier than they used to be?
   > 你觉得现在人们的生活方式比过去更健康吗?
8. `[考生答]` In some ways, yes, since people are far more aware of diet and exercise. That said, we also sit in front of screens far more than before.
   > 某些方面是的,因为人们对饮食和运动的意识强多了。不过话说回来,我们盯着屏幕的时间也远比从前多。

**Part 2 · 考生看图独白**

1. Both pictures capture very different lifestyles, one fast-paced and the other far more laid-back.
   > 两张照片捕捉了截然不同的生活方式,一种快节奏,另一种则悠闲得多。
2. In the first image, a woman is rushing through a busy city street, coffee in hand, clearly in a hurry to get somewhere.
   > 第一张里,一位女士手拿咖啡快步穿过繁忙的城市街道,显然在赶着去某个地方。
3. The second picture, by contrast, shows a man relaxing in a hammock by the sea, with nothing pressing to do.
   > 相比之下,第二张展示一个男人惬意地躺在海边吊床上,没有任何紧要的事要做。
4. The woman in the city might thrive on that energy and the sense of always having a purpose.
   > 城市里那位女士也许正是靠这种活力和时刻有目标的感觉而充满干劲。
5. On the other hand, the man by the sea seems to have made a conscious choice to slow down and appreciate the moment.
   > 另一方面,海边那个男人似乎是有意选择放慢脚步、享受当下。
6. I suspect most of us swing between the two, chasing deadlines during the week and craving peace at the weekend.
   > 我猜我们大多数人都在两者之间摆荡,平日追着截止日期跑,周末又渴望宁静。
7. Personally, I'd lean towards the slower pace, since I believe we do our best work when we're rested rather than run down.
   > 就我个人而言,我更倾向慢节奏,因为我相信人在休息充分而非精疲力竭时才能做出最好的成果。

**考法提示**:**考法** 生活方式是 FCE 常考话题。Part 1 考官会问你的日常作息、如何平衡工作与休息、有没有想改的习惯,回答多用一般现在时+频率副词,并给理由。Part 2 常给「忙碌都市 vs 悠闲慢生活」的对比图,用 whereas / by contrast 对比节奏,结尾表明自己更向往哪种。

**万能句型**:
- `I tend to ___ most days, although ___.` — 谈日常倾向
- `I try to strike a balance between ___ and ___.` — 谈平衡
- `One habit I'd love to change is ___.` — 谈想改的习惯
- `Given the choice, I'd rather ___ than ___.` — 表偏好

### 话题 5:饮食与美食 — Food and Eating Habits
`fce-food`

**Part 1 · 考官问 / 考生答**

1. `[考官问]` Do you enjoy cooking, or do you prefer eating out?
   > 你喜欢做饭,还是更喜欢在外面吃?
2. `[考生答]` I genuinely enjoy cooking when I have the time. There's something satisfying about making a meal from scratch and sharing it with others.
   > 有时间的时候我是真心享受做饭。从头做一顿饭再和别人分享,有种特别的满足感。
3. `[考官问]` Has your diet changed much over the years?
   > 这些年你的饮食变化大吗?
4. `[考生答]` Quite a bit, actually. I've cut down on processed food and started eating far more vegetables than I did as a teenager.
   > 其实变化不小。我减少了加工食品,蔬菜也比青少年时期吃得多多了。
5. `[考官问]` Is there a dish from your country you'd recommend to a visitor?
   > 有没有你家乡的一道菜想推荐给游客?
6. `[考生答]` I'd definitely recommend our dumplings. They're the kind of dish families make together, so they carry a lot of meaning as well as flavour.
   > 我一定会推荐我们的饺子。那是一家人一起包的菜,所以除了味道,还承载着很多意义。
7. `[考官问]` Do you think people pay enough attention to healthy eating?
   > 你觉得人们对健康饮食的关注够吗?
8. `[考生答]` It varies a lot. Some people are very careful, but for many others, busy lifestyles make it far too easy to reach for convenience food.
   > 差别很大。有些人很讲究,但对很多其他人来说,忙碌的生活让人太容易随手就吃方便食品了。

**Part 2 · 考生看图独白**

1. Both photographs show people eating, but the atmosphere around each meal could hardly be more different.
   > 两张照片都展示人们在吃饭,但两顿饭的氛围简直天差地别。
2. In the first picture, a whole family is gathered around a table, sharing dishes and chatting happily over dinner.
   > 第一张里,一家人围坐在餐桌旁,一边分享菜肴,一边愉快地聊着晚餐。
3. The second image shows a young man eating a burger alone at his desk, apparently grabbing a quick bite between tasks.
   > 第二张展示一个年轻男子独自在办公桌前吃汉堡,显然是在工作间隙匆匆填个肚子。
4. The family meal is about far more than food; it's a chance to reconnect at the end of a busy day.
   > 家庭聚餐远不只是吃饭,更是在忙碌一天后重新联络感情的机会。
5. Whereas the man eating alone is probably prioritising convenience, fitting food around a packed schedule rather than the other way round.
   > 而独自吃饭的男子多半是把便利放在首位,让吃饭迁就满满的日程,而非反过来。
6. I imagine both scenes are increasingly common, as modern life pulls us between the meals we'd like and the meals we have time for.
   > 我想这两种场景都越来越常见,因为现代生活让我们在想吃的饭和有时间吃的饭之间挣扎。
7. Personally, I find the shared family meal far more appealing, since eating together nourishes our relationships as much as our bodies.
   > 就我个人而言,我觉得一起吃的家庭饭更有吸引力,因为共餐滋养的不只是身体,还有我们的关系。

**考法提示**:**考法** 饮食是 FCE 常考话题。Part 1 考官会问你的饮食习惯、常做饭还是外食、是否注意健康,回答给出具体例子和理由。Part 2 常给「家庭围坐聚餐 vs 独自吃快餐」的对比图,用 whereas 对比,谈食物在社交与健康中的角色,结尾表个人偏好。

**万能句型**:

### 话题 6:金钱与消费 — Money and Spending
`fce-money`

**Part 1 · 考官问 / 考生答**

1. `[考官问]` Would you describe yourself as a spender or a saver?
   > 你会说自己是爱花钱的人还是爱存钱的人?
2. `[考生答]` Honestly, a bit of both. I'm careful with everyday expenses, but I don't mind splashing out on things that genuinely last.
   > 老实说,两者都有点。日常开销我很谨慎,但对真正耐用的东西,我不介意大方一点。
3. `[考官问]` Do you prefer shopping online or in actual stores?
   > 你更喜欢网购还是逛实体店?
4. `[考生答]` It depends on what I'm buying. For everyday items I shop online for convenience, but for clothes I'd rather try things on in person.
   > 看买什么。日常用品我图方便就网购,但买衣服我还是宁愿到店里试穿。
5. `[考官问]` What's the best thing you've ever spent money on?
   > 你花过最值的一笔钱是什么?
6. `[考生答]` Without a doubt, travelling. I'd rather spend money on experiences than on possessions, because the memories stay with you for life.
   > 毫无疑问是旅行。比起买东西,我更愿意把钱花在经历上,因为那些回忆会伴随你一生。
7. `[考官问]` Do you think young people today are good at managing money?
   > 你觉得现在的年轻人擅长理财吗?
8. `[考生答]` It's a mixed picture. Many are surprisingly savvy about saving and investing, yet the ease of online spending can tempt others into debt.
   > 情况参差不齐。很多人在存钱和投资上出人意料地精明,但网上消费的便利也可能诱使另一些人陷入负债。

**Part 2 · 考生看图独白**

1. Both pictures show people shopping, but they've chosen two quite different ways of going about it.
   > 两张照片都展示人们在购物,但他们选择了两种颇为不同的方式。
2. In the first image, a couple is browsing through a busy shopping centre, carrying bags and clearly enjoying the outing.
   > 第一张里,一对情侣在热闹的购物中心里逛着,提着购物袋,显然很享受这次出行。
3. The second picture, by contrast, shows a woman shopping from her sofa, comparing products on a tablet at home.
   > 相比之下,第二张展示一位女士窝在沙发上购物,在家用平板比较各种商品。
4. The couple at the shopping centre are treating it as a social occasion, something to do together rather than just a transaction.
   > 购物中心里那对情侣把它当成一种社交活动,是可以一起做的事,而不只是一笔交易。
5. The main appeal of shopping online, however, is convenience: you can compare prices instantly and avoid the crowds altogether.
   > 而网购的主要吸引力在于便利:你能即时比价,还完全避开了人群。
6. I'd say each suits a different mood, since sometimes we want the buzz of the high street and other times we just want it done quickly.
   > 我会说两者适合不同的心情,有时我们想要商业街的热闹,有时只想赶快搞定。
7. Personally, I lean towards online shopping for convenience, but nothing quite beats wandering round the shops on a lazy afternoon.
   > 就我个人而言,图方便我更偏向网购,但没什么比得上在慵懒的午后随意逛逛店铺。

**考法提示**:**考法** 金钱消费是 FCE 常考话题。Part 1 考官会问你的花钱习惯、是否存钱、喜欢网购还是逛店,回答要有具体例子和价值判断。Part 2 常给「实体店逛街 vs 在家网购」的对比图,用 whereas 对比两种方式的利弊,谈便利与体验的取舍,结尾表个人偏好。

**万能句型**:
- `I'd describe myself as a fairly ___ spender.` — 描述消费风格
- `I'd rather spend money on ___ than on ___.` — 谈花钱取舍
- `The main appeal of ___ is that ___.` — 说吸引力
- `That said, nothing quite beats ___.` — 转折强调
