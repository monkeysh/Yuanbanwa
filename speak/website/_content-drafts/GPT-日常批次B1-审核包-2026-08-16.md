〖emergency-call·紧急电话〗(补档;已有初级档如下,勿重复)
[已有初级] There's been an accident. / I need an ambulance. / I'm on 5th Avenue near 42nd Street. / There's one person hurt. / He's breathing but not moving. / I'll stay on the line. / Please hurry.
[entry]
  1. I need the police.
  2. Someone broke into my car.
  3. I'm at Third and Main.
  4. Please send someone.
[intermediate]
  1. I'd like to report a car break-in.
  2. I'm in the parking lot at Third and Main.
  3. It just happened — maybe ten minutes ago.
  4. My window is smashed and my backpack is gone.
  5. No one is hurt.
  6. I didn't see who did it.
  7. There's broken glass all over the ground.
  8. Should I wait here for an officer?
  9. My number is 213-555-0142.
[advanced]
  1. I need police right now — someone is breaking into a car in this lot.
  2. I'm at the market on Third and Vermont, and it's happening right now.
  3. He's still there, next to a silver sedan near the back.
  4. I'm inside the store — I haven't gone out there.
  5. He's a man in a black hoodie, maybe thirty years old.
  6. I can't tell if he has a weapon, and I'm not going any closer.
  7. Other people are walking by, but no one has gone near him.
  8. I'll keep watching from the window and tell you if he leaves.
  9. He's just moved toward the back of the lot on foot.
  10. Yes, I can stay on the line until the officers get here.
  11. I'll wave to them from the front door so they can find me.

〖car-breakin·车被砸·车内失窃〗(全新场景) 洛杉矶最高频的一种案子：回到车前发现车窗被砸、包被拿走。报警、拿 DR 编号、叫玻璃上门修。
[entry]
  1. My car window is broken.
  2. Someone took my bag.
  3. I need to call the police.
  4. What should I do now?
[beginner]
  1. Excuse me, did you see what happened to my car?
  2. The passenger window is smashed.
  3. My backpack was on the seat, and now it's gone.
  4. I parked here about two hours ago.
  5. There's glass everywhere — I haven't touched anything.
  6. I'm taking photos before I clean it up.
  7. Are there security cameras in this lot?
  8. Thanks — I'm going to call the police now.
[intermediate]
  1. Hi, I need to report a theft from my vehicle.
  2. It's at the public lot on Third and Main, in Los Angeles.
  3. The window was smashed and a backpack was taken.
  4. No one was in the car, and nobody was hurt.
  5. I don't know who did it, and I didn't see anyone.
  6. Can I file this online, or does an officer need to come out?
  7. The laptop inside has a serial number — I have it written down.
  8. Could you give me the DR number for the report?
  9. My insurance company will ask for that, right?
[advanced]
  1. Hi, my car window was smashed last night — do you do mobile glass repair?
  2. It's the front passenger window on a 2019 Honda CR-V.
  3. The car is drivable, but there's still glass in the door and the seats.
  4. Does the price include vacuuming the glass out?
  5. Is that quote for OEM glass or aftermarket?
  6. My policy may have glass coverage — could you bill the insurance directly?
  7. If I pay out of pocket instead, what's the total with tax?
  8. How soon could someone come out to my address?
  9. Do I need to be there the whole time, or just to hand over the keys?
  10. Is there a warranty on the glass and the installation?
  11. Great — let's book it for tomorrow morning.

〖insurance-claim·保险理赔〗(全新场景) 车被砸、东西被偷、出了事故之后打给保险公司：报案拿 claim 号、问免赔额、约定损、租车、修理厂。
[entry]
  1. I want to report a claim.
  2. My car was broken into.
  3. It happened last night.
  4. What do I do next?
[beginner]
  1. Hi, I'd like to file a claim on my auto policy.
  2. My policy number is on my card — let me read it to you.
  3. Someone broke my car window and took a backpack.
  4. It happened last night in a parking lot.
  5. I already filed a police report.
  6. I have the report number right here.
  7. I also took photos of the damage.
  8. What happens next?
[intermediate]
  1. Hi, I'm calling to follow up on a claim I opened yesterday.
  2. The claim number is 4-8-2-6-1.
  3. Is this covered under comprehensive, or do I need something else?
  4. What's my deductible on this one?
  5. If the repair costs less than that, is it still worth filing?
  6. The backpack and laptop were inside the car — are those covered too?
  7. When will the adjuster contact me?
  8. Can I use my own repair shop, or does it have to be one of yours?
  9. Could you email me a summary of everything we just went over?
[advanced]
  1. Hi, I'd like to go over the estimate the adjuster sent me.
  2. The shop quoted me four hundred more than what's on your estimate.
  3. They said the difference is because your estimate used aftermarket glass.
  4. Could you have the adjuster call the shop directly to work that out?
  5. While it's in the shop, does my policy cover a rental car?
  6. What's the daily limit, and how many days does it cover?
  7. I'd rather not file for the stolen items if it's going to raise my premium.
  8. Could you tell me how this claim will affect my rate at renewal?
  9. If I decide to withdraw part of the claim, is that possible?
  10. Please make a note that I want to be called, not emailed, about next steps.
  11. Thanks — could you send me that in writing today?
